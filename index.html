Korean Dramas

Korean dramas have become more popular in recent years, especially worldwide. It has caught on with the global audience through popular streaming sites such as Netflix, Disney,... Many viewers state that the reason they enjoy Korean dramas is due to the rich storylines, the weekly format that can keep viewers engaged, and the attractiveness of the actors/actresses. Korean dramas also provide a wide range of genres with many unique concepts. Some dramas are also adapted from other countries and other media types.
